Our Work

Our job is to save the surplus and food items that are discarded or even rejected. If you are a restaurant owner, bakery shops owner, hoteliers, food court owners whose food has lost its commercial value such as out of specifications, almost expired, excess stock and close to the date Best Before or Use By but still safe for human use, you can donate it to here for donnes who will make redistribution to people in need from here.

About Us

We aim to rescue surplus food from the restaurant, , bakery shops, food courts and hotel industry; sort and re-distribute it to the Non-Governmental Organizations(NGOs), charity home and welfare center through our strategic partners to reduce food waste and to help to ease and lessen the cost of living of the underprivileged Malaysian.


  • OBJECTIVE

  • To coordinate, plan and carry out the collection of surplus food nationwide.

  • MISSION

  • Food Liberty targets "Zero Hunger" and "Zero Waste" and safe food supply in Malaysia.

  • VISION

  • Become the Leader of Food Bank domestically and internationally by 2025.
